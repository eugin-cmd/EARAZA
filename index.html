    About the conference

    A week of high-level conservation, on one campus

    Join zoo and aquarium professionals from across Eurasia for knowledge sharing, networking, and advancing wildlife conservation.

    The 2026 EARAZA Annual Conference is hosted at Vantara as part of a week-long series of high-level global conservation events.

    Sessions run 26–28 October, with a closed members’ meeting on the 28th and the Global Day plenary on the 29th.

    Our mission

    Cooperation as the working method

    EARAZA aims at strengthening cooperation and developing relations between zoos, oceanariums, breeding centres, exotariums and aquariums of the countries of the Eurasian region.

    Our vision

    Building a global conservation community that protects biodiversity, advances animal welfare and inspires future generations.

    Our mission

    Uniting experts and institutions to advance wildlife conservation through collaboration, knowledge, science, responsible breeding and education.

    Registration information

    No fee. Hosted in full.

    There is no registration fee for EARAZA delegates.

    Who should register?

    • EARAZA member institutions (directors + delegates)
    • Accredited presenters
    • EARAZA committee members
    • Invited observers (by EARAZA Secretariat)

    Up to two attendees per organization.

    Registration Fee

    Free

    • No registration fee for EARAZA delegates
    • All EARAZA members attend free of cost, courtesy of Vantara (India)

    Delegates cover

    • Flights to and from Jamnagar or Rajkot
    • Their own visa — the host provides the invitation letter
    • Medical and travel insurance (mandatory)

    A signed Non-Disclosure Agreement is required before arrival.

    Hospitality & Logistics

    What registration includes

    Everything from the moment you land until you depart.

    • Access to all EARAZA sessions (26–28 October)
    • Access to Global Day plenary (29 October)
    • Conference materials
    • Scheduled internal transport within Vantara
    • Participation in Vantara site-visits (optional sign-up)
    • Food and beverage throughout the conference
    • Accommodation for the duration of the conference
    • Regular transfers from the airport to conference hotels
    • Invitation letters and visa processing support
    • A mall on campus, healthcare facilities, and a swimming pool
